## How it's paid

Some options to react to (numbers are just examples):

- **A. Up front + royalty:** part up front, then a small % of studio revenue each month for a set time, capped (e.g. 5% for 24 months).
- **B. Up front + royalty on growth only:** the % only counts revenue above today's ~$5.7k/mo.
- **C. Up front + Austin marketing:** Austin helps with ads, website and socials for a set time, paid monthly or as a % of bookings it brings in.
- **D. Payments over time:** a fixed price split into monthly payments (e.g. 12 months).
- **E. Referral fee (add-on):** if Austin sends work to the studio later, he gets a % of those jobs.
