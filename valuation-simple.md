**Price for Austin's 75%: ~$32k**

The whole studio is worth about $43k. Pat already owns 25%, so the buyout is 75% of that.

|---|---:|

| Use of the ~$60k build-out under a renewed lease | $10k |

| **Whole studio** | **$43k** |
| **Austin's 75%** | **$32k** |

**Check against earnings:** since July the studio has made about $2.2k a month before anyone is paid, which is about $27k a year. That supports roughly the same price.

**Range:** about $22k with a short lease renewal, up to about $55k if the podcast-services clients stay long term.

**Assumptions**
- The lease renews, and the landlord moves it into the buyers' name and releases Austin, Jeremiah and Beau.
- Cash, card balances and prepaid bookings settle separately at closing.
- This is a rough estimate from the Mercury records, not an appraisal.
