# Amigo Studios valuation (short version)

**Price for Austin's 75%: ~$32k**

The whole studio is worth about $43k. Pat already owns 25%, so the buyout is 75% of that.

| | Low | **Expected** | High |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Gear and furniture (not Pat's own podcast gear) | $12k | **$16.5k** | $16.5k |
| Use of the ~$60k build-out under a renewed lease | $7k | **$10k** | $12k |
| Website and booking system (we both keep resale rights) | $6.5k | **$12k** | $20.5k |
| Peerspace listings and reviews | $3k | **$4k** | $8.5k |
| **Whole studio** | **$29k** | **$43k** | **$58k** |
| **Austin's 75%** | **$22k** | **$32k** | **$43k** |

Each column is its own total. Low assumes a 2–3 year lease renewal and resale value for the gear; High assumes a 5 year renewal and top-end credits.

**Check against earnings:** since July the studio has made about $2.2k a month before anyone is paid, which is about $27k a year. That supports roughly the Expected price. If both podcast-services clients stay long term, earnings could support up to about $55k for the 75%.

**Assumptions**
- The lease renews, and the landlord moves it into the buyers' name and releases Austin, Jeremiah and Beau.
- Cash, card balances and prepaid bookings settle separately at closing.
- This is a rough estimate from the Mercury records, not an appraisal.
